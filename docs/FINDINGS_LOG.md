# Findings log (historical)

This is the README's former TL;DR, moved here verbatim on 2026-10-11 so the
README could state the current status plainly. It is the project's
chronological record of what was tested and found, in the words used at the
time.

> **Read every figure below with this in mind.** On 2026-10-11 it was measured
> that the paper ledger books every entry at the FVG midpoint, a price the
> market never offered at the moment of the signal (+0.52R per trade in the
> backtest, +0.53% of price live). Every PnL figure, win rate and "profitable
> N/6 segments" claim below was computed with that bias, and nearly all of them
> at a 0.1% fee instead of the real 0.3822%. Absolute figures are optimistic.
> Relative comparisons between arms that took different numbers of trades are
> biased toward the arm with more trades. See the TL;DR in `README.md` and
> `experiments/entry_fill_realism.py`.
>
> Several entries below were themselves later superseded, and say so inline
> (for example "Live LONG is statistically proven profitable", later shown to be
> confounded by a +28.7% rally, then to fail the deflated Sharpe test, then to
> vanish at real fills).

- **Run it:** `python main.py` — one command starts everything (data streams, GNN, SMC/FVG analysis, dashboard at `http://localhost:8080`). Requires Neo4j running first.
- **No real money at risk right now.** There are two separate decision paths, both simulated:
  1. **SMC/FVG entry-insight + $10k virtual paper-trading ledger** — read-only dashboard recommendations, scored against live price with a fixed-risk simulated account. This is the path with real backtested evidence behind it.
  2. **Multi-agent LLM consensus (Bull/Bear/Critic) + Binance testnet orders** — a separate, independent path (`strategy/agents.py` → `consensus.py` → `execution.py`). Places real orders, but only on Binance's testnet (fake funds). **If this path is ever pointed at a real account:** it already places real, exchange-side `STOP_LOSS_LIMIT`/`TAKE_PROFIT_LIMIT` orders (GTC) at entry time — protection lives on Binance's own matching engine, not in this process, so a laptop shutdown does not leave a position unprotected. Position state also persists to disk across restarts. Unexercised in practice, though — it has never actually closed a real trade, even on testnet (see below).
- **What's actually validated:** a 2-year backtest (`backtest.py`) shows win rate holding in a 55–61% band across 6 different market regimes — not just one lucky window. Position sizing is fee-aware and capped by a portfolio-level correlation risk cap. A separate target/exit backtest matrix (`experiments/backtest_target_matrix.py`) confirmed the live FVG-gap-based target is already the best of 7 tested variants at 5m/15m on expectancy *and* drawdown — wider targets (1-2x the gap, or fixed 2R/3R/5R) are strictly worse, monotonically, the wider they get.
  - **~70 backtest configurations across 11 technical-analysis confluence filters (VWAP, EMA, RSI, MACD, Stochastic, CCI, Bollinger, ADX, volume, OBV, Donchian), their combinations, and 5 standalone crossover/breakout strategies — none beat the SMC/FVG baseline once tested across a full 2-year, 6-regime window.** Standalone TA strategies with no SMC/FVG involvement lose money outright where baseline earns $200-290K — the strongest evidence yet that the structural entry logic is doing real work. See `experiments/full_ta_robustness_validation.py`, `experiments/combo_multiregime_robustness.py`, `experiments/standalone_ta_strategies_robustness.py`, full detail in `RESEARCH_NOTES.md`.
- **What's not yet proven:**
  - **GNN's contribution to Path 1 was measured as negligible, and re-confirmed (2026-08-30) with the fixed/retrained checkpoint over a long multi-regime window.** Original ablation (SMC-only vs SMC+GNN, identical win rate/trade count) carried an asterisk after the checkpoint-corruption bug (below) raised the possibility it was silently measuring a degraded zero-signal checkpoint. Re-ran `backtest.py --with-gnn` against the now-stable, fully-retrained checkpoint over 730d/1h (139,824 candle-replays): **57.5% win rate both arms, −$969 PnL delta on ~$297K — still negligible, asterisk resolved.** GNN confluence measurably adds no edge to Path 1 with either a broken or a healthy checkpoint. Not a reason to remove GNN from the architecture (Path 2's LLM debate still reads it), but Path 1's actual driver is SMC/FVG, not GNN. Full detail in `RESEARCH_NOTES.md`.
  - **A SPOT-EXECUTABLE configuration exists, and its drawdown is 5x better (2026-09-08).** Path 1 sizes as risk/stop-distance, so notional/balance = risk% × conviction / stop% — tighter stops mean MORE implied leverage, and peak TOTAL notional across concurrent positions reached **17.2x the account**, placeable on spot only 24% of the time. Cutting risk does not help (a 4x cut left peak leverage at 16.5x, because `PORTFOLIO_MAX_CORRELATED_RISK_PCT` caps RISK and the freed budget just admits more positions: 3,364 → 8,015 trades). A notional cap alone does not help either (at 1.0x a single production-sized position already exceeds the account, leaving 14 trades in two years). **Both together do: risk 0.25% + `PORTFOLIO_MAX_NOTIONAL_MULTIPLE=1.0` gives 2,656 trades, 57.9% WR, profitable 6/6 segments, 100% spot-executable, worst-segment drawdown 9.7% (median 8.5%)** — against 69.1%/43.2% leveraged. In absolute terms +$39,367 on $10,000 over 730d (+394%). **This qualifies the 2026-09-07 "drawdown is structural" finding**: that was measured on the leveraged configuration, and the drawdown turns out to be largely a leverage artifact — the lever that reaches it is the notional cap, which did not exist at the time. Nothing deployed; the cap defaults to 0. See `experiments/spot_viable_risk_sizing.py` and `experiments/notional_cap_spot_viability.py`.
  - **Drawdown is STRUCTURAL — three independent risk levers exhausted, and LONG-only is RUIN (2026-09-07).** Sizing has no effect (drawdown %% is scale-invariant under fixed-fractional sizing; 42.7%% -> 42.5%%). Dropping SHORT is catastrophic: **LONG_ONLY worst-segment drawdown 126.7%%** — past zero, i.e. ruin — vs 68.5%% with shorts included, so shorts DAMP the book rather than drag it. Tightening `PORTFOLIO_MAX_CORRELATED_RISK_PCT` destroys returns far faster than drawdown (0.06 = 51%% of PnL for 1.5pp; 0.04 makes drawdown WORSE at 95.3%% by removing diversification; only 0.03 reaches 39.8%%, at 2%% of returns). Nothing cleared the pre-registered gate at any setting. **This also corrects the 2026-08-30 SHORT-removal note**, which recorded "LONG_ONLY robustly loses" — it does not, it is profitable 6/6 segments; that measurement ran with the (since shown inert) GNN signal and before slippage was modelled. "Keep SHORT" survives, but for **survival, not profitability**. Remaining options are to accept ~68%% worst / ~43%% median drawdown with small capital, change the signal side, or not deploy. See `experiments/{long_only_viability,correlation_cap_drawdown_sweep}.py`.
  - **The GNN is RETIRED on evidence (2026-09-07).** The earlier ablation ("adds no edge", 57.5% both arms) never tested what it was read as testing: probing the checkpoint over 2,000 real predictions showed output scale ~±0.017 (1σ) against `GNN_CONVICTION_THRESHOLD = 0.08` — 4.6σ out, so `_apply_gnn_confluence` returned early on **98.9% of calls**. It measured a gated-out signal, not an uninformative one. Re-ran properly on the full 730d/6-segment bar with the gate moved to where the signal actually speaks (0.035 ≈ 2σ, 0.017 ≈ 1σ, and 0.0 = ungated): **win rate 57.4% at every threshold including ungated**, and nothing cleared the pre-registered gate. The signal carries no usable information for Path 1, and failed even with look-ahead contamination in its favour. See `experiments/gnn_threshold_ablation.py`.
  - **CONFOUNDED — "live LONG is proven profitable" was measured inside a 28.74% market rally (2026-09-08).** The most-cited result here (live LONG n=64, block-bootstrap 95% CI [+$102.04, +$284.20]) does not mean what it was reported to mean. Over the live window 2026-08-13→09-08 **every one of the 8 watchlist symbols rose, median +28.74%** (BTC +24.2%, ETH +32.1%, SOL +36.0%, DOT +39.8%, XRP +38.5%). In that market a long-biased strategy wins largely regardless of edge and a short-biased one loses regardless of edge — which is exactly the observed split (LONG 73.4% WR vs its own 56.7% backtest; SHORT 36.8% vs its 55.8% backtest). **The methodological error:** a block bootstrap asks "is mean PnL different from zero", which is nearly free to pass in a rising market and cannot separate edge from beta. **The corrected benchmark:** strategy +43.75% vs buy-and-hold +28.74% over the same window = **+15.01pp**, so real edge does survive — stronger evidence than the CI ever was, but far smaller than "profitable, CI above zero" implied. Two limits: the ledger risks a fixed unit and is never fully invested while buy-and-hold is 100% exposed (not like-for-like on capital), and this is one strongly bullish month. **Rule going forward: every live-performance claim needs a BENCHMARK, not just a significance test against zero.** External context: independent studies put raw SMC hit rate at 38-45%, which our live SHORT (36.8%) matches and our live LONG (73.4%) does not.
  - **Live LONG is statistically proven profitable; live SHORT is NOT yet proven unprofitable (2026-09-07).** Moving block bootstrap (block=5, 20k resamples, so overlapping correlated trades are handled honestly): crypto LONG n=57, +$190.87/trade, 95% CI [+$118.86, +$309.28] — **profitable**. Crypto SHORT n=37, −$215.54/trade, CI [−$453.59, **+$30.42**] — **straddles zero**. Combined SHORT n=44 likewise. ~120 short trades are needed for significance, about 76 more than we have (~50 days). Turning SHORT off now would be acting on an absence of evidence. See `experiments/short_live_evidence_power.py`.
  - **The live/backtest SHORT gap is a SELECTION problem, and six mechanistic explanations were tested and rejected (2026-09-07).** On the matched window only **9 of 37 live shorts (24%)** correspond to a backtest short; on those 9 outcomes agree 8/9 and entry prices differ by a median 0.047%, so **execution is fine**. Live took 28 shorts the backtest never proposed (−$4,227) and missed 43 it did (+$4,093). Rejected: the GNN signal (gated out in both arms), forming-candle entry (*helps* shorts, 65.9% vs 60.8%), a cold analyzer on restart (backfill rebuilds SMC **and** FVG), testnet data contamination (backfill returns production data, verified to the cent), FVG memory truncation across 39 restarts (LONG −1.6pp, SHORT +0.4pp), and intra-candle re-evaluation (*helps* shorts, 68.5% vs 56.4%). Per its own pre-registration the hunt **stops here** — the answer needs ~76 more live shorts, not a seventh hypothesis. See `experiments/short_setup_attribution.py` and the four `*_effect.py` scripts.
  - **Known defect: a stopped-out setup can be re-entered immediately, because the ledger and the analyzers are fully decoupled (2026-09-07).** `backtrack/paper_trading.py` holds no reference to `fvg_analyzer`/`smc_analyzer`, so when a trade stops out nothing marks the zone as failed — the order block stays active and keeps being proposed. Found by inspecting the ledger: three consecutive TSLA longs on 2026-09-04 each closing at exactly **−$526.08** within one hour, the identical setup re-entered after every stop-out (the same pattern the code already documents for NVDA on 2026-08-19). `FairValueGapAnalyzer` already has `mitigated`/`invalidated` fields; nothing feeds them. **Not fixed, deliberately:** re-entries are net POSITIVE overall (n=40, +$1,315) and blocking them would discard $2,873 of LONG profit to avoid $1,558 of SHORT loss. Direction-aware only: LONG re-entries 65.4% WR/+$2,873, SHORT re-entries 42.9% WR/−$1,558.
  - **Drawdown is the real barrier to real money, and position sizing CANNOT fix it (2026-09-07).** At the measured 0.119% slippage the strategy is profitable 6/6 segments and never ruins an account, but carries **worst-segment drawdown 68.5%, median 42.7%**. Two levers were tested against a pre-registered gate (worstDD<40, medDD<25, PnL>=50% of base, profitable>=5/6): equity-based sizing and a drawdown halt. **Nothing cleared it.** Equity sizing keeps 96% of PnL but moves median drawdown 42.7%→42.5% — because drawdown % is scale-invariant under fixed-fractional sizing: halving positions halves gains and losses alike, leaving the equity curve's shape unchanged. The halt does cut drawdown, but only by switching the system off (HALT_25: trades 3,366→389, PnL to 7% of baseline, profitable 2/6) — these drawdowns happen early and often, so the breaker trips and stays tripped. **Drawdown here is a property of the return distribution, not of risk management**; the only levers that could move it change WHICH trades are taken, i.e. the strategy itself. Separately found: **Path 1 has no loss limit at all** — `DAILY_DRAWDOWN_LIMIT` is enforced only in `strategy/execution.py` (Path 2, disabled) — and `risk_amount` was sized off `starting_balance`, so a fixed $200 unit becomes 6.3% of what's left after a 68.5% drawdown. Both new settings (`PAPER_TRADING_EQUITY_BASED_SIZING`, `PAPER_TRADING_MAX_DRAWDOWN_HALT_PCT`) default off; nothing deployed. Full detail in `RESEARCH_NOTES.md`.

All hosts resolve to real AWS IPs (not sinkholed), and it is locally licensed. **This removes the "no reachable venue" blocker on the real-money goal**, and the port is cheap since the codebase is already Binance-shaped.   - **A structural GNN checkpoint-corruption bug was found and permanently fixed (2026-08-28).** The model's architecture was built from whichever Neo4j edge types happened to have >=1 edge at retrain time — the daily graph-pruning job fully emptying a low-count type (SUPPLIES, REGULATES) made the next retrain produce an incompatibly-shaped model, silently falling back to a zero signal until noticed. Root-fixed in `gnn/dataset.py`/`gnn/trainer.py` — the model's parameter shape is now independent of which edge types are currently populated. Both checkpoints retrained against the stable architecture and verified loading cleanly. Full forensics in `RESEARCH_NOTES.md`.
